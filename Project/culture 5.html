Indian clothing is as diverse as the country's culture, with a wide range of traditional garments and styles. The clothing in India varies not only by region but also by gender, age, and occasion. Here are some of the most prominent types of clothing in India:

Sari (Saree):

The sari is one of the most iconic and widely recognized traditional garments in India. It consists of a long piece of fabric, typically six to nine meters in length, draped elegantly around the body. Saris come in a wide range of fabrics, colors, and designs. Blouses are worn with saris.
Salwar Kameez:

The salwar kameez is a popular traditional outfit for women, especially in North India. It consists of a long tunic (kameez) paired with loose-fitting trousers (salwar) and a matching or contrasting scarf (dupatta).
Lehenga Choli:

Lehenga choli is a traditional outfit often worn by women on special occasions like weddings and festivals. It includes a flared skirt (lehenga), a fitted blouse (choli), and a long scarf (dupatta).
Anarkali Suit:

The Anarkali suit is characterized by its long, frock-style top with fitted leggings or churidar. It is a popular choice for formal events and celebrations.
Ghagra Choli:

Ghagra choli, similar to the lehenga choli, consists of a flared skirt (ghagra) paired with a choli (blouse) and a dupatta. It is commonly worn in North India.
Dhoti and Kurta:

Dhoti is a traditional garment for men that involves a piece of cloth wrapped around the waist and legs. It is often worn with a kurta (a long tunic) and a scarf (uttariya).
Sherwani:

The sherwani is a formal outfit for men, often worn during weddings and special occasions. It is a long, button-down coat with intricate embroidery, typically paired with churidar or dhoti.
Bandhgala Suit:

The bandhgala is a formal suit for men, featuring a high-collared jacket with buttons. It is commonly worn for formal occasions.
Turban (Pagri):

Turbans are traditional headwear for men, and their style and color can vary by region and community. Turbans are often worn with formal outfits or as a symbol of cultural identity.
Kurta Pajama:

The kurta pajama is a popular and comfortable outfit for men. It consists of a long tunic (kameez) and straight-cut pants (pajama). It is worn casually and can also be part of formal attire.
Pagdi (Turban):

Turbans are a symbol of identity and culture in various regions of India. Different communities and regions have their distinct styles and colors of turbans.
School Uniforms:

In India, school uniforms are a common sight, and they vary by school and region. School uniforms are typically worn by students as part of their daily attire.
Western Clothing:

Western-style clothing is also widely worn in India, especially in urban areas. Jeans, T-shirts, and formal suits are commonly worn by both men and women.
